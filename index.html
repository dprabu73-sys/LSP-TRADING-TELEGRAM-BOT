    FAQ

    Honest answers for beginners

    📱 What if my phone dies during a live trade?

    Nothing bad. The robot lives on a cloud computer in Mumbai (AWS), so it keeps watching prices and will use your profit exit or safety net even if your phone is off.

    💸 Can the robot lose all my money in one trade?

    No. Each trade’s risk is capped at 25–35 points, and the daily safety shield stops everything once your ₹5,000 daily loss budget is used.

    ⏱️ How does the robot know when to sell?

    By strict rules. When your +25 point target hits it sells in about 50 ms. If the market keeps climbing, the profit stairs trail higher to protect extra gains.

    🔒 Why does it ask me twice for Live Mode?

    Because real money is sacred. Asking twice means you can’t switch to live trading by accident while the phone is in your pocket.

    🎮 Paper Mode vs Full Mode?

    Paper Mode uses pretend points for learning. Full Mode uses your real Zerodha cash on the real NSE exchange.

    ☕ Can I stop early if I’m happy with the profit?

    Yes. Tap /squareoff to take the money home, then /scan_stop to let the robot rest.

    🏦 Where does my profit go?

    It stays in your regulated Zerodha account. You can withdraw it to your bank any time from Kite.
